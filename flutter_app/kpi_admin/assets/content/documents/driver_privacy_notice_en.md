# Privacy notice for the use of road safety technologies and driver safety tools

Last updated: [Datum – vor Go-live eintragen]

With this privacy notice we inform you about how we process your personal data in connection with our camera-based road safety system installed in delivery vehicles and with driver safety tools ("road safety technologies").

The system does not record continuously. It only creates event-triggered image recordings (photos) without audio, which are triggered by a detected safety-relevant event. There is no livestream, no gapless surveillance and no continuous recording or transmission. No audio recording takes place at any time – neither continuously nor on an event.

## 2. Categories of personal data

We may process the following categories of personal data about you:
- identification data (e.g. names, images);
- contact data (e.g. email address);
- event-triggered image recordings (photos) without audio, triggered by a detected safety-relevant event (e.g. exterior views of the road environment and interior views of the vehicle cabin); no continuous video recording takes place, and no audio recording takes place at any time;
- vehicle operating data (e.g. location, speed, acceleration, braking, cornering, distance to the vehicle ahead);
- safety incident data (e.g. detected unsafe driving behaviour, including speeding, harsh braking, acceleration and turning, collisions, tailgating, distracted driving, drowsiness, camera obstruction, forward collision warnings, rolling stop detection, as well as violations of traffic signs or traffic lights);
- interaction data (e.g. your interactions with the road safety technologies system and the app, including any obstruction of the camera).
- In exceptional cases we may process health data about you (e.g. visible injuries in the event of a road traffic accident).

## 3. Purposes

We may process your personal data for the following purposes:
- to improve road safety, including the safety of our drivers and other road users, and to prevent harm to our company;
- to analyse road traffic incidents and to assess legal claims; and
- to comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal bases

We process your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

We process your personal data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our legitimate interests are:
- protecting the health and physical integrity of our drivers and other road users (e.g. pedestrians);
- reducing legal risks (e.g. legal claims), financial risks (e.g. increased insurance premiums or repair costs) and reputational risks (e.g. the perception that delivery vehicles are driven recklessly) arising from road traffic accidents; and
- analysing road traffic accidents and handling legal claims effectively.

Where we process your personal data to comply with legal obligations, we rely on Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

In the event of a serious road traffic accident, we may in exceptional cases process health data (e.g. visible injuries). In such cases we process these special categories of data because this is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Art. 9(2)(f) GDPR).

## 5. Categories of recipients

Where this is necessary for the purposes described above, we may disclose your personal data to
- our road safety technologies provider, which supplies the camera hardware, the cloud platform and the mobile application intended for the driver that are required to operate the road safety technologies,
- authorities and public bodies (e.g. courts, law enforcement agencies) in the context of legal proceedings, and
- insurers and contractual partners (including their lawyers), where this is necessary to assess road traffic incidents or to handle legal claims.

## 6. Transfers to third countries

We generally process your personal data within the EU/EEA. Where we transfer your personal data to countries outside the EU/EEA ("third countries"), such as to our road safety technologies provider in the United States, we generally rely on standard contractual clauses (you can find the text of the standard contractual clauses here: https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en) pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR. In addition, we have put in place supplementary safeguards to ensure an adequate level of protection for your personal data, in particular encryption of the data in transit and at rest.

For further details on our data transfers to third countries, or for an extract of this notice, please contact us using the contact details set out in section 9 of this privacy notice.

## 7. Retention periods

We do not store your personal data for longer than is necessary for the purposes described above. The specific retention periods depend on the purpose of the processing:
- To improve road safety: we store all event-triggered image recordings held on the local devices in the delivery vehicles for a few days [konkrete Frist – vor Go-live eintragen], and all image recordings stored in our provider's cloud (which relate exclusively to detected safety incidents) for a few weeks [konkrete Frist – vor Go-live eintragen], before both are deleted automatically.
- To investigate road traffic accidents and to assess legal claims: we store the relevant personal data for the duration of the applicable limitation period, including until the full settlement of any related legal disputes and any appeal proceedings, as well as for any further period required by law or necessary for post-settlement matters.

Once our applicable retention periods have expired, we will delete or anonymise your personal data. For further information about our retention practices, please contact us using the contact details set out in section 9 of this privacy notice.

## 8. Sources of personal data

We collect personal data directly from you only if you do not object to the use of our road safety technologies (opt-out). Your participation in the road safety technologies is voluntary. If you decide at any time not to participate in the road safety technologies (see section 9 of this privacy notice), this will not result in any disadvantage for you.

## 9. Data subject rights and questions

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:
- Right of access to personal data: you have the right to obtain access to your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR);
- Right to rectification: you have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected by us, or incomplete personal data completed (Art. 16 GDPR);
- Right to erasure: you have the right to have your personal data erased by us (Art. 17 GDPR);
- Right to restriction of processing: you have the right to have the processing of your personal data restricted (Art. 18 GDPR);
- Right to object: you may object to the processing of your personal data at any time (Art. 21 GDPR).

In addition to your right to object under Art. 21 GDPR, we grant you the right to object to the road safety technologies at any time. You do not have to give any reasons for doing so. Exercising your right to object will not result in any disadvantage whatsoever in your employment relationship. If you exercise your right to object, we will stop processing your personal data, unless the processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

If you would like to exercise these rights or have questions about this privacy notice, please contact us at [konkrete Kontaktdaten]. Please note that your rights may be restricted in certain situations. In such a case we will inform you of the reason for the restriction.

If you have concerns about the processing of your personal data, you are welcome to contact us. Irrespective of this, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority pursuant to Art. 77 GDPR.
