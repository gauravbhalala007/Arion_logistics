## 2. Categories of personal data

We may process the following categories of personal data about you:

image and sound recordings of the road traffic accident, including metadata (e.g. date, time and place of the recording); and

personal data collected at the scene of the accident (e.g. your name, your address, your contact details, your vehicle's licence plate and insurance details).
- The vehicles are equipped with camera-based road safety technology and driver safety tools. The system does not record continuously: recordings are created exclusively on an event-triggered basis, when a safety-relevant event or an accident is detected; image and sound are recorded in the process. No continuous picture or sound recording takes place. In doing so, outward-facing cameras may incidentally capture images of other road users and record their voices. However, people's faces and other vehicles' licence plates are automatically obscured (pixelated) in this image material. Non-pixelated image material is only retained and reviewed if a road traffic accident has occurred or comparable circumstances exist.

## 3. Purposes

We may process your personal data for the following purposes:

to investigate road traffic accidents;

to handle legal claims (including insurance claims); and

to comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal bases

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"):

We process your personal data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our legitimate interests consist in reducing legal risks (e.g. legal claims) and financial risks (e.g. increased insurance premiums) arising from road traffic accidents. Where we process your personal data to comply with legal obligations, we rely on Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

In exceptional cases we may process health data about you (e.g. visible injuries). In such cases we process special categories of personal data because this is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Art. 9(2)(f) GDPR).

## 5. Categories of recipients

Where this is necessary for the purposes described above, we may disclose your personal data to

our vehicle technology provider;

authorities and public bodies (e.g. courts, law enforcement agencies) in the context of legal proceedings; and

insurers and counterparties (including their lawyers or other legal advisers), where this is necessary to investigate road traffic accidents or to handle legal claims.

## 6. Transfers to third countries

We generally process your personal data within the European Union (EU) or the European Economic Area (EEA). Where we transfer your personal data to recipients in countries outside the EU/EEA ("third countries"), such as to our vehicle technology provider in the United States, we generally rely on standard contractual clauses (you can find the text of the standard contractual clauses here: https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en) pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR. In addition, we have put in place supplementary safeguards to ensure an adequate level of protection for your personal data, in particular encryption of the data in transit and at rest.

For further details on our transfers of data to third countries, or for copies of these safeguards, please contact us using the contact details set out in section 9 of this privacy notice.

## 7. Retention period
- We do not store your personal data for longer than is necessary for the purposes described above.
- In particular, where the road traffic accident gives rise to a potential legal or insurance claim, we may retain the relevant personal data for the duration of the applicable limitation period. This includes the full settlement of any related legal disputes and any appeal proceedings, as well as any further periods required by law or necessary for post-settlement matters.

Once our applicable retention periods have expired, we will delete or anonymise your personal data. For further details on our retention practices, please contact us using the contact details set out in section 9 of this privacy notice.

Event-triggered image and sound recordings that are not connected with a road traffic accident remain locally on the device and are automatically overwritten within a short period [konkrete Frist – vor Go-live eintragen].

## 8. Sources of personal data

We collect personal data either directly from you or from authorities, public bodies, insurers or other third parties involved in handling the road traffic accident (e.g. legal advisers). You may provide us with personal data voluntarily at the scene of the accident. This does not affect any obligations you may have under applicable law or under contracts with third parties (e.g. with your insurer).

## 9. Data subject rights and questions
- Under the GDPR you may have the following rights:

Right of access to personal data: you can request access to your personal data from us (Art. 15 GDPR);

Right to rectification: you can ask us to correct inaccurate personal data or to complete incomplete personal data (Art. 16 GDPR);

Right to erasure: you can request the erasure of your personal data (Art. 17 GDPR);

Right to restriction of processing: you can ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data (Art. 18 GDPR);

Right to object: you may object to the processing of your personal data at any time (Art. 21 GDPR).
- If you would like to exercise these rights or have questions about this privacy notice, please contact us at [spezielle Kontaktdaten]. Please note that your rights may be restricted in certain situations. In such a case we will inform you of the reason for the restriction. If you have concerns about the processing of your personal data, we ask you to let us know so that we can try to address your concerns. Irrespective of this, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority pursuant to Art. 77 GDPR.
